## 🥊 Two types of sessions

### 1. 👤 Séance privée

**1 coach → 1 cliente**

Example:

```text
Private Kickboxing
1 personne
60 min
```

The coach can have:

```text
09:00 → Available
10:00 → Available
11:00 → Available
```

When one woman books **10:00**, that slot becomes unavailable for another private booking.

```text
10:00
❌ Private session booked
```

---

### 2. 👥 Séance en groupe

**1 coach → plusieurs participantes**

For example:

```text
Group Kickboxing
Maximum: 10 personnes
60 min
```

The calendar should show:

```text
Monday 17 August

18:00
👥 Group Kickboxing
7 / 10 places available

19:30
👥 Group Kickboxing
10 / 10
FULL
```

If Sara books:

```text
7 / 10
```

it becomes:

```text
8 / 10
```

Another woman can still book the same session.

Only when:

```text
10 / 10
```

should the system show:

**Complet**

---

# 🗄️ Database design

This changes your database slightly.

I would have a `session_types` table:

```text
session_types
─────────────
id
name
type
description
duration
max_participants
price
active
```

For example:

```text
1 | Kickboxing privé | PRIVATE | ... | 60 | 1  | 300 DH
2 | Kickboxing groupe| GROUP   | ... | 60 | 10 | 150 DH
```

Then you need actual scheduled sessions:

```text
sessions
────────
id
session_type_id
date
start_time
end_time
status
```

And reservations:

```text
reservations
────────────
id
session_id
client_id
status
created_at
```

The important relationship is:

```text
Session Type
     │
     ├── Private
     │      ↓
     │   Session
     │      ↓
     │   1 reservation
     │
     └── Group
            ↓
         Session
            ↓
       1 → 10 reservations
```

---

# 🔥 This gives you much more flexibility

For example, the coach could create:

```text
Monday

09:00 → Private → max 1
10:00 → Private → max 1
17:00 → Group → max 10
18:30 → Group → max 10
20:00 → Private → max 1
```

And the calendar automatically calculates availability.

### Private

```text
Capacity = 1
Booked = 1

1 - 1 = 0

❌ Unavailable
```

### Group

```text
Capacity = 10
Booked = 7

10 - 7 = 3

✅ 3 places remaining
```

Or:

```text

[ 17:00 ]

[ 60 min ]

Nombre maximum
[ 10 ]

Prix
[ 150 DH ]

[Créer la séance]
```

Then the dashboard shows:

```text
📅 Aujourd'hui

09:00  👤 Private
       Sara
       🟢 Confirmée

17:00  👥 Group
       8 / 10 participantes
       🟢 2 places restantes

18:30  👥 Group
       10 / 10
       🔴 Complet
```

### One important point

Don't make **"Private" and "Group" just a filter on the calendar**. They should be a real concept in your database because their **capacity, booking logic, and availability rules are different**.

That will make your reservation system much easier to extend later—for example, if she eventually offers **women-only groups, children's groups, different prices, recurring classes, or multiple coaches**.
